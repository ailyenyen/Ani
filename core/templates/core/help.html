How do price alerts work?

Ani will send you a message when your chosen crop reaches the price you set.

  1. Tap Price Alerts, then Add Price Alert
  2. Choose a crop and type your price
  3. Choose above or below
{% icon 'bell' 20 %} Add Price Alert

What do ↑ and ↓ mean?

↑ 5.2% means the price is 5.2% higher than about one week ago.

↓ 1.5% means the price is 1.5% lower than about one week ago.

All prices are in pesos per kilo (₱/kg).

Where do the prices come from?

{% if live_reports %}

Ani downloads PDF reports linked from the official DA Price Monitoring page. Open a crop's details to view its exact report, reporting period, and original price row.

  • DA Bantay Presyo: weekly average consumer retail prices for NCR, not individual market quotations or farmer buying offers.
  • Kadiwa: program rice prices published in the same DA report. These are a separate product with outlet and eligibility restrictions; this connection is not a complete Kadiwa outlet feed.
  • DA Bantay Presyo regional portal: market-level crop and rice prices, with region/category dates. See the Compare Markets for available regions, source comparisons, and lowest-price rankings.
  • Infanta LGU: crop retail prices and ranges at Infanta Commercial Center, Quezon. Unspecified grades remain separate from DA specifications.

Report dates are the end of each reporting week. Missing weeks are not filled with invented daily prices. Prices older than 14 days are excluded from current listings. Collection runs when the importer is executed; browsing does not trigger a refresh.
