How can we help?

Simple answers for using Ani.

How do I check a crop's price?

  1. Tap Market Prices
  2. Choose your crop
  3. Select your location
{% icon 'wheat' 20 %} Go to Market Prices

How do I compare markets?

  1. Tap Compare Markets
  2. Choose your crop
  3. Select your location
  4. Look at the prices. The highest price is shown first.
{% icon 'compare' 20 %} Go to Compare Markets

How do price alerts work?

Ani will send you a message when your chosen crop reaches the price you set.

  1. Tap Price Alerts, then Add Price Alert
  2. Choose a crop and type your price
  3. Choose above or below
{% icon 'bell' 20 %} Add Price Alert

What do ↑ and ↓ mean?

↑ 5.2% means the price is 5.2% higher than about one week ago.

↓ 1.5% means the price is 1.5% lower than about one week ago.

All prices are in pesos per kilo (₱/kg).

Where do the prices come from?

Ani collects prices from public sources. Every price in Ani shows its source, so you know where it came from.

  • DA Bantay Presyo: price monitoring by the Department of Agriculture
  • Kadiwa: Kadiwa center price lists
  • Local market bulletins: prices posted by public and local markets

Prices are a guide. The price you are offered can be different because of quality, size and quantity.
