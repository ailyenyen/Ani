How it works

Three steps. No tutorial needed.

  1. 1

    Choose your crop

    Pick a crop and your province. Ani shows the latest price right away.

  2. 2

    Compare markets

    See every market's price in one view and find out where your crop sells highest.

  3. 3

    Get an alert

    Set your target price once. Ani tells you when it's reached, so you know when to sell.
